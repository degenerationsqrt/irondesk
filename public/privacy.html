IronDesk Android

Health data & privacy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

IronDesk uses Health Connect only after you choose to connect it. Access is read-only and can be revoked at any time from Android Health Connect settings.

Data IronDesk can read

With your permission, IronDesk reads daily totals or summaries for steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep duration, weight, body-fat percentage, total calories burned, exercise duration, and VO2 max. IronDesk does not request medical-record permissions, exercise routes, raw location, or background health access.

How the data is used

The data is used to show fitness and recovery trends inside IronDesk. Health Connect exercise time is not converted into a detailed IronDesk workout, which avoids duplicating activities already imported from Garmin FIT or CSV files.

Storage and transfer

Daily Health Connect summaries are stored with your other IronDesk data on the device. If you explicitly enable Personal Cloud Sync and sign in, those summaries can be stored in your private Firebase user document so they can appear on your other signed-in devices and the IronDesk website. Raw Health Connect sensor records do not sync to Firebase.

IronDesk does not sell health data, use it for advertising, or share it with Crew members. Firebase processes opt-in cloud data as IronDesk's service provider.

Your controls

  • Use Manage Access in IronDesk Settings to grant or revoke Health Connect permissions.
  • Turn off foreground auto-sync without revoking Health Connect access.
  • Use Remove imported summaries to delete Health Connect summaries and derived Health Connect bodyweight entries from IronDesk.
  • Pause Personal Cloud Sync separately at any time.

A synchronized deletion marker prevents an older cloud copy from automatically restoring removed Health Connect summaries. Data remains until you remove it, clear local app data, or request deletion of the associated hosted account data.

Questions and deletion requests

Open a request through the IronDesk GitHub Issues page. Do not include private health measurements in a public issue.
